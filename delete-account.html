Delete your account - Nearby Job Finder

You can delete your Nearby Job Finder account and your data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Read what will be erased. Deletion cannot be undone.
  5. For a Google account, tap Continue with Google and select the same Google account you used to sign in. This confirms your identity and starts deletion. Older phone-login accounts use Send code, then an SMS code and Delete forever instead.
  6. Keep the app open until deletion finishes and you are logged out. If it fails, use Try again or contact us; do not assume deletion is complete.

If you cannot open the app

Email chanduchess100@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Use the Google email address you used to sign in and identify your profile. For an older phone-login account, include the registered phone number. We may ask for enough information to verify ownership before deleting data. Never send a password or a one-time sign-in code. We reply when the request has been handled.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, area, age, email, address, bio, photo, skills, business name.
  • Your phone number (private contact record).
  • Your "free today" availability card and your blocked list.
  • Every job you posted, with its photos, its responses, the phone numbers shared on it and its chat. If a seeker was holding one of your jobs, they get a "back on the list" alert first.
  • Your own chat messages and your responses (with your phone number) on jobs you held or completed as a seeker. A job you were holding goes back to the open list.
  • Your login (Firebase Authentication account).

What stays, and why

  • Reports you filed about other people stay so we can keep people safe. They retain your internal user ID and the report details, which may include text you supplied. Your profile is deleted, but these reports are not anonymous.
  • A job you completed as a seeker stays in the poster's history, showing the name you used then. The poster still controls that job record.
  • If someone else blocked you, their blocked list keeps a small entry (your ID and the name you used) until they unblock you.
  • Ratings you gave other people stay as part of their star totals (the total number only, not who gave them).
  • Chat messages you sent on jobs you withdrew from earlier (before this feature) and old responses on such jobs may remain until the poster's account is deleted.
  • Copies in backups kept by our hosting provider (Google Firebase) can take some time to age out. We do not control that schedule.

If you want any of these removed, email chanduchess100@gmail.com.
