Privacy Policy - Nearby Job Finder

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Nearby Job Finder ("the app") is a local marketplace for short, urgent jobs. People who need help ("posters") post a job, and people nearby ("seekers") accept it. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

Contact: chanduchess100@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Google sign-in - Firebase Authentication uses your Google account to sign you in and stores your account ID and email. The app can use your Google display name to help set up your profile. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Phone number - you type a contact number in your profile. This number is not verified by SMS and is not used for Google sign-in. It is stored privately and shared only between the poster and confirmed seeker on a job.
  • Profile details - name, role, area, skills, available days, and optional email, age, address, bio, business name and photo. Profile fields other than the private phone number are stored in a profile readable by signed-in users. Do not put sensitive information in your profile or job description.
  • Location - your phone's GPS location, used to show you jobs near you and to place a job you post on the map for seekers near it.
  • Availability cards - if you turn on "I'm free today", your name, area, skills, note and location (if added) are visible to signed-in users.
  • Jobs you post - title, description, pay, schedule, and the job's location. These are visible to signed-in users; nearby filters run in the app.
  • Chats - messages between a poster and the seeker who took the job. Only those two people can read them.
  • Ratings - ratings posters and seekers give each other after a job.
  • Blocks and reports - blocks are used to filter jobs in the app. Reports (including any note you write) are stored for the app owner to review; reporting does not yet automatically hide a job.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show your phone number to all users - only to the matched pair on a confirmed job.
  • We do not collect your contacts, files, or anything outside the list above.
  • We do not use advertising or ad trackers.

Where the data lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) on Google's servers. All traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access is controlled by security rules: private details (phone numbers, chats) are readable only by the people involved.

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap services. For road-distance estimates, the app sends the seeker and job coordinates to the public OSRM routing service. These services receive the network request, including the IP address. If you open Directions, Google Maps receives the destination; if you choose WhatsApp or a call, that app receives the number you selected.

Location

The app asks for location permission so it can show nearby jobs and let you pin a job on the map. Job locations you post are visible to other users so they can find work near them. We do not track your location in the background for anything else.

Children

The app is for adults arranging paid work. It is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile > Delete my account. For Google accounts, tap Continue with Google and choose the same account to confirm deletion. Older phone-login accounts use an SMS code instead. Keep the app open until deletion finishes. Your profile, private contact, availability card, blocked list and posted jobs (including their photos, responses and chats) are erased. Some records held by other users remain; see the Delete account page for details and the email request option.

Changes
